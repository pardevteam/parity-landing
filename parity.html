$PAR is access, not equity.

Holding $PAR doesn't give you a claim on any company or a promised return — it unlocks how deep into the market you can see, and gives you a say in what Parity lists next.

  • Depth & alerting
    Full order-book depth, redemption-time tracking, and custom price/spread alerts sit behind $PAR — the top-line price feed stays free for everyone.
  • Listing governance
    Holders vote on which new issuers and instruments get added, and how heavily custody risk is weighted in each trust score.
  • Usage rewards
    Keep a portfolio connected and contribute verified price or liquidity reports, and Parity distributes $PAR for that participation — a usage reward, not a yield on holding the token.
